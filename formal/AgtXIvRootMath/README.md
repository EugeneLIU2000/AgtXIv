# AgtXIv Root Mathematics (Lean 4)

This project kernel-checks the selected load-bearing mathematical interfaces of
the three conceptual Root PaperAgents:

1. Gottesman's phase-aware Pauli common-fixed-space construction;
2. Veitch et al.'s pure stabilizer atoms and their convex hull;
3. Howard and Campbell's full robustness-of-magic optimization.

`ROOT_MATH_KERNEL_COMPLETE` means that this selected cross-root mathematical
chain is closed in Lean. It does **not** mean that the three papers have been
formalized page by page, that their source and physical semantics have received
human acceptance, or that the target Stabilizerness paper and its numerics are
verified.

## Kernel-checked mathematical chain

### Gottesman root

- Concrete commuting, involutive, independent signed Pauli generators produce
  the binary stabilizer group; group cardinality and the Pauli character are
  derived, not assumed.
- For `r` generators on `n` qubits, the invariant space of their generated
  binary subgroup has dimension `2^(n-r)`. The usual `n-k` generator form
  therefore has dimension `2^k`.
- For `r=n`, generator-by-generator common `+1` fixedness is proved equivalent
  to subgroup invariance. Normalized group averaging is then Hermitian,
  idempotent, positive semidefinite, trace one, and rank one. It is an exact
  pure density projector, and normalized fixed vectors differ only by global
  phase.

Physical picture: every independent compatible `+1` condition halves the
allowed Hilbert-space dimension. Starting from dimension `2^n`, `n`
independent conditions leave one ray.

### Gottesman-to-Veitch bridge

- A Clifford witness is constructed for every complete signed Pauli frame; it
  is not supplied as an assumption.
- The construction completes the binary symplectic frame, builds coherent
  syndrome read/flip states, obtains a unitary change of basis, and proves its
  conjugation action on every phase-aware Pauli.
- Consequently, complete-frame common-eigenspace projectors are exactly the
  semantic Clifford orbit of the computational zero projector.
- Their finite atom sets and ordinary real convex hulls are equal.

Physical picture: the commuting generators are "read-bit" observables and
their symplectic partners are "flip-bit" observables. Keeping both fixes an
entire quantum coordinate system, so the change of basis is a genuine
Clifford rotation rather than an arbitrary eigenbasis choice.

### Howard--Campbell root

- Actual all-qubit stabilizer projectors affinely span every trace-one
  Hermitian operator. Feasible signed decompositions therefore exist for all
  density matrices.
- Finite-dimensional compactness proves that the minimum absolute coefficient
  weight is attained; no minimizer is assumed.
- Full robustness equals one exactly on the Clifford-orbit stabilizer
  polytope, and is greater than one exactly outside it.
- If an exact real-linear map sends every pure stabilizer atom into the output
  stabilizer polytope, Lean constructs the row-stochastic coefficient kernel
  and proves deterministic full-robustness monotonicity. The kernel is not an
  input assumption.

The key intuition is

```text
absolute coefficient weight = 1 + 2 * total negative weight.
```

Ordinary probabilities have cost one. Negative pseudo-probability must be
balanced by additional positive mass, and classical random mixing cannot
increase that cancellation cost.

## Exact boundaries

The following are not inherited from `ROOT_MATH_KERNEL_COMPLETE`:

- source-text and convention alignment or human physical-semantic acceptance;
- equivalence between the semantic Pauli normalizer and a chosen gate syntax
  generated by `H`, `S`, and `CNOT`;
- a proof that every stabilizer protocol in Veitch's operational list
  implements the exact atom-preserving map interface;
- selective/measurement-branch monotonicity on average;
- fixed-window reduced robustness, the target paper's graph-theoretic closed
  form, or any experimental/numerical result;
- finite shots, noise, floating-point error, or another physical
  approximation model.

An unformalized source/semantic bridge is not called a physical approximation.
No physical approximation is introduced anywhere in this Root project.

## Rebuild and audit

From this directory, with `<repo>` replaced by the repository path:

```text
ELAN_HOME=<repo>/.tools/elan <repo>/.tools/elan/bin/lake build
ELAN_HOME=<repo>/.tools/elan <repo>/.tools/elan/bin/lake env lean Audit.lean
```

The audit is required to report no `sorryAx`, no project-defined axiom, and
only Lean/Mathlib's standard logical dependencies `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
