### 2.2 The gap that this delivery closes

The three layers above do not connect. The web flow stops at source-linked candidates with all
six assessment axes hard-coded to `NOT_ASSESSED` (`tools/export_web_library.py:49`). The Lean
that genuinely checks things is not reachable from that flow. `schema v0.1` specifies the glue
and, apart from `handoff/`, does not execute it.

### 2.3 What `schema v0.1` actually contains

Nine callable work templates over 23 operations across eight agent modules, each with
`AGENT.md`, `INTERFACE.md`, `CONFORMANCE.md`, a JSON Schema and an offline checker. The
contracts themselves are small and clean: `task.schema.json` is 2.2 KB, `result.schema.json`
2.0 KB. `IMPLEMENTATION.md` states plainly that no runtime host exists;
`PENDING_TESTS.md` lists thirteen interface gaps `G-01` … `G-13`, most untested because there
is no execution entry point.

`storage/graph-contract.schema.json` defines `ProjectionRequest`, `QueryRequest` and
`QueryResult` with three bounded query kinds, and `Provenance.backend` is an enum of exactly
`["SEALED_EXPORT_REFERENCE", "NEO4J"]`. The file-backed reference backend is a first-class
citizen of the contract, not a fallback.

## 3. The worked example

### 3.1 Why arXiv:2607.26154v1

- It is current research (2026-07), not a settled classic, so the system is shown working on
  live material.
- Three distinct fields meet in it. `agents/graph-theoretic-nonstabilizerness/agent.json`
  records three imports, each with an `assumption_match` review:
  `contract:2602.18939v1:reduced-polytope-vrep` (quantum information),
  `statement:foundation:perfect-graph-weighted-duality` (graph theory),
  `statement:foundation:finite-lp-strong-duality` (optimization). The paper's contribution is
  the bridge between them.
- Its material is complete enough to demonstrate every layer: frozen source with byte anchors
  (`source/anchors.jsonl`), 21 claim statements, reasoning chains, a 74-node dependency DAG,
  kernel-checked Lean declarations, and four explicitly recorded blockers.
- Most importantly, **its main theorem is not proved**, and the system says exactly why.

### 3.2 The central figure

The upstream closure of the paper's main theorem `claim:closed-form-rom` (layer 8) is
**29 nodes across 9 layers and 5 branches**:

```
layer 8   claim:closed-form-rom                                        [closed_form]
layer 7   claim:exact-graph-program                                    [optimization]
layer 6   claim:relaxation-exactness   claim:relaxed-mwis-dual
layer 5   claim:sign-alignment-identity ✅   claim:perfect-graph-antiblocker
          claim:relaxed-lp-dual   claim:no-active-free-signs
layer 4   claim:relaxed-affine-span   claim:pauli-active-dependency
layer 3   claim:mwis-definition ✅   claim:perfect-graph-definition ✅
          claim:exact-reduced-vrep   claim:sign-relaxed-polytope   (+3)
layer 2   claim:frustration-graph   claim:reduced-rom   claim:reduced-stabilizer-polytope
layer 1   claim:full-rom   claim:full-stabilizer-polytope   claim:pauli-window
layer 0   ⛔ root:perfect-graph-weighted-duality      [graph_theory]
          ⛔ root:varela-reduced-polytope             [reduced_polytope]
             foundation:finite-lp-strong-duality      [optimization]
             root:gottesman-stabilizer-formalism
             root:veitch-stabilizer-resource-theory
             root:howard-campbell-rom                 [quantum information]
```

This single figure carries motivation, architecture, demonstration and vision at once:

- Six foundations from three fields, nine layers, one theorem at the top.
- Three rungs are kernel-checked, and **they sit in the mathematics branches**
  (`mwis-definition` and `sign-alignment-identity` in optimization,
  `perfect-graph-definition` in graph theory) while feeding a quantum-information result.
  Cross-field reuse is visible without being asserted.
- Two of the six foundations are blocked, and the figure makes it obvious that they are
  load-bearing.
- **No Lean source appears in the figure.** Declarations are evidence attached to nodes, seen
  only when a node is opened.

### 3.3 The demonstration's climax

Not "an axis turns green." Rather:

> Three sub-claims are promoted to `KERNEL_CHECKED`, **the main theorem stays `BLOCKED`**, and
> the system names both blockers on the spot.

```
claim:sign-alignment-identity  → AgtXIv.Stabilizerness.max_abs_signed_sum       KERNEL_CHECKED
claim:mwis-definition          → AgtXIv.GraphFoundation.maxWeightIndependent    KERNEL_CHECKED
claim:perfect-graph-definition → AgtXIv.GraphFoundation.IsPerfect               KERNEL_CHECKED
claim:closed-form-rom          → main theorem                                   BLOCKED
    ├─ blocker:2607.26154v1:perfect-graph-foundation-unchecked   (OPEN)
    └─ blocker:2607.26154v1:root-contract-not-accepted           (OPEN)
```

The first blocker is the talk's sharpest point. `foundations/perfect-graph-weighted-duality.json`
records a 1975 Chvátal result that the paper uses as background, with
`source_fidelity: UNCHECKED`, `acceptance_basis: DECLARED_BACKGROUND`,
`statement_alignment: PENDING` and `formalization_mode: EXPLICIT_THEOREM_PARAMETER` — assumed
in Lean, not proved, and the system declares this rather than hiding it.

**The bottleneck to verifying a 2026 quantum-information paper is a 1970s graph-theory theorem
that has never been frozen from its primary source and aligned to the conventions in use.**
That sentence is the shortest available argument for the whole project, and the example says it
rather than the speaker.

An all-green demonstration convinces nobody. Being able to state precisely what is blocked, why,
and by whom is what earns credibility with this audience.

## 4. Components to build

### C1 — `utility.formal-check` as a real host operation (1–1.5 days)

Extend the pattern proven by `handoff.py` with a second operation. The handler is a **fixed
program**, `lean-kernel-check/1.0`, not a model — which is itself one of the talk's arguments
(the Utility Agent has no scientific discretion).

It runs `lake build` and `lake env lean Audit.lean` in `formal/AgtXIvStabilizerness`, captures
the real exit code, declaration list, axiom audit and placeholder scan, and emits a receipt
conforming to `Utility Agent/formal-check-receipt.schema.json`, bound to `request_hash`. The
host persists Task bytes, attempt, execution events, Result and artifact hashes under the same
transactional discipline already demonstrated.

The receipt carries `scope_warning` and `scientific_acceptance_effect` verbatim. Delivery is not
scientific acceptance, and that boundary lives in the data rather than on a slide.

**Design decision:** do not use `schema v0.1/host_reference/`. It depends on Pydantic AI and has
never been executed; adopting it inside five days is unnecessary risk. It appears in the talk as
the stated next step.

### C2 — `SEALED_EXPORT_REFERENCE` graph backend (1 day)

Implement `graph-contract.schema.json`: a `ProjectionRequest` over the sealed batch, and a
`QueryRequest` of kind `DependencyQuery` returning a `QueryResult` with
`provenance.backend = "SEALED_EXPORT_REFERENCE"`, bounded by `Limits`.

The query it must answer is the demonstration's climax: *within this exact batch, what does
`claim:closed-form-rom` load-bearingly depend on, and what is each dependency's state?*

`InferenceQuery` and `ContextQuery` are implemented if time allows; otherwise they return an
explicit unsupported outcome. An empty result must never stand in for an unimplemented one. The
Neo4j adapter is attempted only if 2026-09-20 has slack; the contract makes the backend
swappable and the talk says so.

### C3 — Web surface reads `schema v0.1` artifacts (1.5 days)

Three pieces:

1. **Chain view.** `tools/export_web_library.py:60` currently builds the full reader view only
   for `slug == 'robustness-of-magic'`; 2607.26154 is present merely as a historical pilot
   reference. Build the layered chain view for the 29-node closure: nodes ordered by layer,
   coloured by branch, each carrying its state.
2. **Axis binding.** Replace the blanket `NOT_ASSESSED` at `tools/export_web_library.py:49`
   with per-claim binding read from the formal-check receipt. Two axes stay separate:

   | Axis | New state | Who answers it |
   |---|---|---|
   | `mathematical_correctness` | `KERNEL_CHECKED` | the Lean kernel — a machine fact |
   | `formal_alignment` | requires a **signed** `review.alignment` | a domain expert, named |

   Recording one signed alignment judgement is recommended. It makes visible, at the same
   moment, what the machine checked, what a person judged, and who is accountable — which is
   the direct answer to "how is this different from an LLM making things up."
3. **Provenance panel.** Per node: `task_id`, `agent`, `operation`, `attempt_id`,
   `principal_id`, `cost_units`, receipt hash.

### C4 — The talk (0.5 day of design, already done here; 1 day of production)

The structure (§8) and the vision content (§9) are settled in this document, which is the
majority of the intellectual work. What remains on 09-22 is producing slides against that
structure and rehearsing to time. The outline is confirmed on 09-16 so that any structural
problem surfaces early rather than on the last day.

## 5. Data flow

```
Stabilizerness/arXiv-2607.26154v1/draft.tex        frozen bytes, sha256
  └─ source anchor (byte span)
      └─ claim node in the 74-node DAG             content hash, revision, layer, branch
          ├─ Task{agent: utility, operation: utility.formal-check}
          │    └─ handler lean-kernel-check/1.0        fixed program, no scientific discretion
          │         └─ formal-check-receipt            declarations, axiom audit, no sorry, env pins
          │              └─ axis mathematical_correctness = KERNEL_CHECKED
          ├─ Task{agent: review, operation: review.alignment}
          │    └─ alignment-draft                      producer may not be the reviewer
          │         └─ axis formal_alignment
          └─ projection → QueryResult(DependencyQuery) → chain view
```

The structural claim of the talk: **every arrow is a contract, and a contract's work is to
forbid.** The Utility Agent cannot make scientific judgements. A reviewer cannot be the producer.
A backtranslator cannot see the source. The derived graph is never a second write path.

## 9. Vision — four directions

**A standing statement, repeated on every slide in this section.** The distance between the
current implementation and any of these four directions is large. None is a roadmap item with a
date. Each requires sustained work by a community rather than by one project, and each is stated
here with what exists today and what is missing, so that the gap is visible rather than implied.

### 9.1 Reuse

**The vision.** When proving a new theorem, determine which already-verified upstream
declarations can be extended to it. This carries two consequences beyond convenience.

*Mathematical theorems and physical semantics become tightly coupled.* Because the six-axis
separation forbids a Lean theorem from ever standing in for a physical statement, the physical
content — model assumptions, approximation regime, the observable actually measured — must be
written down explicitly instead of living between the lines. This is the role of
`SemanticContract`.

*The unit of formalization changes.* The prevailing practice is to formalize one isolated paper,
producing thousands of lines of Lean that no human reads or navigates. Here, what a human reads
is a **dependency chain**, built layer by layer, each rung carrying a name, its conditions, its
source anchor and its state. Lean declarations are evidence attached to rungs. Formalization
stops being an opaque wall of code and becomes a visible, incrementally constructed structure.

This reframes the relationship to existing formalization libraries: **they provide the terrain,
this provides the map.** What those projects lack is not tooling but an answer to "which one
should be proved next, and what does proving it unlock."

**What exists today.** `agent.json` records three imports, each with an `assumption_match`
review. `AgtXIvStabilizerness` audits three reused graph-theory declarations inside a
quantum-information target. `root_standardization_priority` already records a build order with
written reasons for five roots. The 74-node DAG is layered and branched.

**Distance.** Large. No automated search for reusable upstream declarations exists;
`dependency.search` is specified, and the only executing path is a bounded local bibliography
scan in `handoff/`. Matching an upstream declaration's conditions to a new target is a human
judgement today. `SemanticContract` is specified in `AgtXIv.md` §5.7 and has no producer. The
chain view does not exist yet — it is built this week for one paper.

**What a community would have to do.** Maintain a shared, versioned registry of verified
declarations carrying machine-readable conditions. Agree conventions for weight, sign and
normalization alignment across fields. Reach coverage at which "is there something upstream I
can reuse" has a non-trivial answer more often than not.

### 9.2 Knowledge increment

**The vision.** Measure what a new theorem actually contributes to the knowledge base, against a
fixed baseline, without claiming world-first.

**What exists today.** `agent.json` has a `local_delta` section. The Lean status is literally
`TARGET_LOCAL_DELTA_KERNEL_CHECKED`. The DAG separates inherited roots from new claims, so
"what is new here" is readable from structure. The Delta Agent contract states its rule
directly: compare against a fixed baseline, do not claim world-first.
`delta-draft.schema.json` and its checker exist.

**Distance.** Large. The Delta checker currently only requires the comparison set and the
assigned targets to intersect — recorded as gap `G-07`. Identity assembly that would make an
independence claim meaningful is not wired — gap `G-08`. No actual comparison has been run on a
real pair of results. "Contribution" at present means structural difference within one frozen
batch, which is not scientific significance.

**What a community would have to do.** Agree what a baseline means within a field. Assemble a
corpus large enough that the baseline is not arbitrary. Find reviewers willing to sign delta
assessments and be accountable for them.

### 9.3 Knowledge discovery

**The vision.** Three questions. Which theorems matter in a field. By what route were those
theorems obtained. And whether the pattern of connections can be learned well enough to propose
new theorems worth proving.

**What exists today.** Importance is computable from the graph: out-degree within this DAG gives
`claim:frustration-graph` 10 and `root:gottesman-stabilizer-formalism` 9.
`root_standardization_priority` records five priorities with reasons. Edges carry a `type`, and
multi-premise inference is represented by an explicit `via_step` node rather than being lost as
an unrepresented hyperedge, so "by what route" is recoverable. 74 nodes span 13 branches.

**Distance.** Very large, and the three questions are not equally far away. One paper's DAG is
not a field, and degree within a single hand-built DAG is not importance. The second question is
partly answered by existing structure. The third — learning connections in order to generate new
theorems — is an **open research question, not a roadmap item.** Its precondition is that enough
claims are verified and condition-carrying that the connections are worth learning from;
learning from candidate edges would be learning from noise. This is precisely why the graph must
remain derived and must never become a second write path: the trustworthiness of a connection
cannot exceed that of the records it connects.

**What a community would have to do.** Build many papers' DAGs against a common contract.
Establish cross-paper node identity. Publish honest negative results about which connections did
not generalize.

### 9.4 Structured transparency, against black-box exploration and compute asymmetry

**The vision.** Reduce the black-box character and the randomness of AI-driven knowledge
exploration, and prevent the ability to *check* a result from becoming a function of capital.

**Context (September 2026).** OpenAI reported a proof concerning the Navier–Stokes equations
produced by roughly 10,000 agents running for 88 hours on an internal model, and stated it does
not intend to claim the Millennium Prize. A credit dispute followed. Tristan Buckmaster (NYU)
states that OpenAI offered him sole authorship conditional on removing Levent Alpöge, an
Anthropic researcher, and that he refused; shortly before OpenAI's announcement, Buckmaster and
Alpöge had released a solution to the closely related Euler equations. OpenAI denies wrongdoing,
states that no person or model there had seen the other work and that its solution differs,
while conceding that de-identified product data may have improved its models. These are the
parties' stated positions and should be presented as such.

**The structural point, not a slogan.** If checking a result requires owning comparable compute,
then verification itself becomes a function of capital. A byte-anchored, structured record
decouples checking from compute ownership.

State the cost honestly, because the qualifier matters and the audience will ask. Re-checking
needs a one-time setup: downloading Mathlib's prebuilt cache, a few gigabytes over a few
minutes (`lake exe cache get`). On this machine that cache occupies 11 GB — 10 GB of it
compiled Mathlib, against 104 MB of Mathlib source, and the three Lean projects here share one
copy. **After that setup, re-checking this result is 84 seconds on one laptop.**

A few gigabytes and a few minutes is within reach of any graduate student. Ten thousand agents
for 88 hours is not. That is a difference in kind, not in degree — but it is the accurate
version, and this project does not get to make unqualified claims. The asymmetry that matters
is not who can produce; it is **who can audit**.

**What exists today.** A `Task` freezes its visible inputs; a `Result` records real execution
attribution. Every claim carries byte anchors and content hashes. Fields such as
`source_fidelity: UNCHECKED` force the system to declare what it has *not* checked rather than
leaving the omission silent. And the Delta Agent's rule — compare against a fixed baseline, do
not claim world-first — was written into this project before the dispute occurred. That is a
design principle predating the event, not a comment on it.

**Distance.** Large. The host runtime executes one operation. No model runs inside these
contracts yet. Independent identity assembly, evidence gates and formal admission are specified
and unimplemented (`G-08`, `G-09`). And nothing here constrains a system that simply declines to
adopt such contracts: adoption is a question of community norms, not of technique.

**What a community would have to do.** Journals, referees or funders would have to ask for
records of this kind. Enough groups would have to adopt them that a result arriving without a
traceable chain reads as incomplete rather than as normal.
