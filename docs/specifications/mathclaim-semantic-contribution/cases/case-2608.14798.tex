\subsection{Case 2608.14798: resource measures and ensemble scope}\label{case:contrib-14798}

This quantum-information slice contains definitions, exact formulas, asymptotic results, resource bounds, and an independently identified random-versus-pseudorandom transfer gap.

The stabilizer partition function and core partition function are related definitions, not duplicate claims. Exact Haar averages and their asymptotic forms also remain separate: the latter is valid only under a declared scaling regime. A mathematics view may connect them by implication, but it must retain the approximation envelope.

The strongest pruning counterexample concerns the full-support subset-phase-state claims. The source frames them for a pseudomagic SPS/PRF ensemble, whereas the checked derivation available to the study establishes exact formulas for uniformly random Boolean functions. The mapping is therefore partial and reduction is blocked. Mathematical verification of the narrower fixture cannot erase the source's actor, access, and ensemble conditions or silently transfer the result to a cryptographic setting.

The case also separates a bundled statement of stabilizer-work properties from a specific subadditivity theorem. Contribution analysis records introduction of the resource measure and proof of particular properties as different operations. Counting both as arbitrary claims overstates granularity; collapsing them into one result hides proof coverage. The contribution unit and its baseline make the distinction explicit.
