\begin{abstract}
This document derives a first-version interface for source-faithful scientific and mathematical claims. It separates textual claim identity from discourse classification, evidence, dependency, verification, and mathematical normalization. The design is tested against a reproducible random sample of ten theoretical-physics papers first submitted to arXiv between 1 and 25 August 2026. Every normative requirement is tied to literature evidence or to at least two cross-topic stress-test cases.
\end{abstract}
