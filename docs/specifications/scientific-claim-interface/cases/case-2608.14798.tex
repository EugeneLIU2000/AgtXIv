\paragraph{Difficult split and scope examples.}
The SPF is an analytic generating object,
\[
\mathcal Z_\beta(\psi)=e^{-\beta}\sum_{P\in\mathcal P_n}
\cosh\!\left(\beta\operatorname{tr}(\psi P)\right),
\]
but it does not retain the labelled signed spectrum. A normalization must distinguish ``encodes every even moment'' from ``reconstructs every labelled Pauli expectation''. The Clifford-orbit and SRE expansions are equivalent representations, not new physical effects.

The sampling claim combines statistical and truncation errors. For fixed $k$ and $\beta$, the supplement gives
\[
\mathcal N=O\!\left(
\frac{\cosh^2\beta}{e^{2\beta}{\bigl(\epsilon-\Delta(\beta,k)\bigr)}^2}
\log\frac1\delta\right),\qquad \epsilon>\Delta(\beta,k).
\]
Consequently, ``efficiently estimable'' cannot be stored without $k$, $\beta$, normalization, additive error, failure probability, and the truncation remainder.

The resource claims are scoped to deterministic pure-state stabilizer protocols. The interconversion theorem is
\[
\psi^{\otimes n}\xrightarrow{\mathfrak F}\phi^{\otimes m}
\Longrightarrow
\mathcal W_\beta(\phi^{\otimes m})
\leq\mathcal W_\beta(\psi^{\otimes n})
\leq n\mathcal W_\beta(\psi),
\quad \beta\neq0.
\]
The source separately shows that curve dominance is not sufficient for conversion. The implication direction and free-operation class are therefore part of the claim identity.

The hydrogen claims require model-indexed scope. STO-3G and 6-31G reverse the ordering between $R=0.5$ and $R=6$ bohr. The records preserve exact diagonalization, basis, Jordan--Wigner encoding, and the authors' refusal to infer basis-independent molecular magic.

\paragraph{Source-fidelity decisions.}
The machine records quote exact TeX lines rather than rendered prose. Main-text theorem statements are consolidated with supplemental proofs only when the conclusion is the same. Supplemental-only results, including the discrete SPS density and finite-sample validation, remain separate candidates. Imported results are attributed to other work even when used centrally. Claims about pseudorandom SPS are not silently strengthened to uniformly random Boolean functions; this mismatch remains visible as interface pressure. Property bundles are retained only where the paper itself presents them as one resource-theoretic profile, and their non-atomicity is marked.

\paragraph{Normalization boundaries and interface pressure.}
MathClaimIR is well suited to closed forms, inequalities, limits, and probability bounds. It is partial for ensemble sampling prescriptions, algorithms, cryptographic indistinguishability, free-operation classes, and computational experiments. The stress test requires a minimal interface to separate mathematical normalization from: (i) source modality and attribution; (ii) parameter and asymptotic scope; (iii) object kind, including definition, theorem, algorithm, observation, and limitation; (iv) model and representation choices; and (v) relations among a main theorem, proof, example, and application. A single ``claim type'' enum would be too rigid because one candidate may be both a definition and an extension boundary, or both an empirical observation and a model-sensitive interpretation.

\paragraph{Provisional verdict.}
A concise ScientificClaim core remains viable, but only as a source-grounded assertion with structured scope and modality. MathClaimIR should be optional and subordinate: it captures the dense exact mathematics well, yet cannot by itself represent cryptographic observer models, estimator regimes, pure-state protocol restrictions, or numerical basis dependence. These belong in annotations, relations, or assessments rather than being discarded or forced into one formula.
