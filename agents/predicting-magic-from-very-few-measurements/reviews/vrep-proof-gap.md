# V-representation proof gap

The theorem statement remains plausible, but the current appendix proof is not sufficient for an accepted AgtXIv root export.

At `pra_version.tex` lines 716–720 the proof claims that, because a stabilizer group is full rank, its signed intersection with the measurement set is a maximal commuting subset. This is false. Take two qubits, `M = {Z₁, X₂}`, and the stabilizer state `|00⟩`. Its stabilizer intersection with `±M` contains `Z₁` but not `X₂`; nevertheless `{Z₁, X₂}` is a larger commuting measurement context. The projected point `(1,0)` is consequently not a vertex: it is the midpoint of `(1,1)` and `(1,-1)` in the square generated by the maximal context.

Lines 728–732 then try to prove extremality, but the set-difference direction is inconsistent and the coordinate comparison does not handle a `-1` coordinate.

## Repair route

1. Show that every admissible maximal signed commuting context extends to a full stabilizer group, so its `v_(S,f)` is physically reachable.
2. Start from any pure stabilizer projection whose measured support is not maximal. Add a commuting measured Pauli not fixed by the state, split into its `+1` and `-1` eigenextensions, and average them. The new coordinate cancels while the old coordinates remain. Iterate to express the original projection as a convex combination of admissible maximal-context vertices.
3. For a maximal-context candidate vertex, every existing coordinate is `+1` or `-1`. Equality in a convex combination forces every contributing point to carry the same saturated coordinates; its support contains the maximal context and therefore equals it. This gives extremality without the faulty intersection claim.

Until that repaired argument is written and checked, the root contract remains `BLOCKED` even though source fidelity is `PASSED`.
